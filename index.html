📥 Upload sheet music

Skip the typing: upload the music and the app does the rest.

  • 🎼 MusicXML (.musicxml, .mxl) or ABC (.abc): turned into a song with string + finger numbers, playback and highlighting. You can check it below before saving.
  • 📄 PDF or photo: the app reads the notes from the page, and keeps the original to read along with. Clear, computer-made sheet music reads best; always check the notes before saving.

How to type notes
  • String + finger: A0 = open A string, E1 = first finger on E.
  • Longer notes: add :2, :3 or :4 beats. Quick notes: :0.5.
  • Bar lines: put | between bars (or leave them out and they're added for you).
  • Rest: R (or R:2 for a 2-beat rest).
  • Low/high 2nd finger: chosen from the key; force it with E2L (G) or E2H (G♯).
